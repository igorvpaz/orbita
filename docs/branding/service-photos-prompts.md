# Fotos ilustrativas dos serviços

Geradas com a ferramenta integrada de imagens, sem fallback. Cenas fictícias para demonstração. Cada foto é reutilizada nas unidades que oferecem o mesmo serviço. Arquivos otimizados em `apps/web/public/services/`.

## cafe-da-manha

Arquivo: `/services/cafe-da-manha.webp`

Prompt final:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: fictional service catalog photo for Órbita, a coworking training app in Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil. Primary request: a ceramic cup of espresso, fresh pão de queijo, sliced papaya and a small bread basket on a light oak coworking café table. Editorial commercial photography, realistic materials and textures, warm neutral palette with subtle olive and gold accents. Landscape composition, main subject centered with room for a wide card crop. No people, no readable text, no logos, no watermarks. This is an illustrative fictional scene, not a photo of a real business.
